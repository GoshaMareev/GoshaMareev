# George Mareev

### AI/ML Engineer · Building & deploying AI agents and AI platforms

I design, build, and ship production-grade **AI agents** and the **platforms** they run on — from
retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and multi-agent architectures to self-hosted LLM inference,
evaluation, and observability. My focus is turning large language models into **reliable, measurable
products** that deliver real business value, including in **data-sensitive, on-prem environments**.

---

## 🚀 What I do

- 🤖 **AI agents in production** — RAG, retrieval, tool use, and feedback loops built on a shared multi-agent core
- 🧠 **Self-hosted & local LLM inference** — Ollama and OpenAI-compatible endpoints with pluggable embeddings and reranking
- 🔎 **Retrieval & vector search** — multi-format document indexing, metadata-aware retrieval, ChromaDB / FAISS
- 📊 **Evaluation & quality** — A/B agent comparison, confidence scoring, analytics dashboards, and continuous feedback
- 🔐 **Secure deployment** — SSO / identity proxying, environment isolation, authentication, and automated backups
- ⚙️ **MLOps & data workflows** — reproducible pipelines, experiment tracking, and big-data processing

## 🛡️ Guardrails & AI safety

- 🚧 **Grounded outputs** — retrieval-grounded answers with source attribution and confidence scoring to curb hallucination
- 🧾 **Input & output validation** — schema-based validation and sanitization at system boundaries, with prompt-injection hardening
- 🔏 **Data protection** — self-hosted / on-prem inference so sensitive data never leaves the environment, with PII-aware handling
- 🪪 **Access control** — authentication, SSO identity proxying, per-agent environment isolation, and least-privilege secrets
- 📉 **Quality monitoring** — confidence thresholds, low-rated-answer tracking, and feedback loops to catch regressions

## 🌱 Currently exploring

- Agent orchestration and tool-calling patterns for complex, multi-step workflows
- LLM evaluation & observability across quality, latency, and cost
- On-prem / GPU inference optimization and quantized model serving

## 🤝 Open to collaborate on

- Applied AI/ML and agentic systems with measurable business impact
- RAG pipelines, LLM platforms, and AI product MVPs
- Research, hackathons, and interdisciplinary AI initiatives
